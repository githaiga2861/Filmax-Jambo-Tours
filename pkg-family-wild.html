The Journey

Kenya Through Your Children's Eyes

Children who experience the wild before they experience the world — who learn the name of a giraffe before the name of a brand, who learn patience watching a lion before they learn impatience in a classroom — carry that knowledge differently. It reshapes them.

Family Wild is designed with this in mind. Every element — from the child-friendly Nairobi introductions to the Junior Ranger programme in the Mara — is calibrated to create wonder safely, joyfully, and unforgettably. No compromises on luxury. No compromises on wildlife. Simply Kenya, made accessible to every member of your family.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Day 01

Nairobi: Introduction to Kenya's Wild

Nairobi · Giraffe Centre · DSWT

Begin in Nairobi — the world's only capital city with a national park inside it. Morning at the Giraffe Centre, where Rothschild giraffe (one of Africa's most endangered) lean down from their platform to accept pellets from your children's outstretched hands. Afternoon at the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust elephant orphanage — baby elephants playing in red mud, their keepers in green coats, the bond between them one of the most moving things in Africa.

  • Giraffe Centre visit
  • Giraffe feeding experience
  • DSWT elephant orphanage
  • Nairobi National Park afternoon drive
Day 02

Nairobi National Park · Nairobi Museum

Nairobi National Park

Nairobi National Park — lions against a city skyline — is a concept that still astonishes adults. For children it is simply magic. Game drive through the park in the morning. Afternoon at the Karen Blixen Museum (for the parents) or the Nairobi National Museum's natural history collection (for the children), which includes a life-size dinosaur cast and Kenya's most complete collection of ethnic artefacts.

  • Nairobi NP game drive
  • Lion with city backdrop
  • Museum visit
  • Nairobi city tour
Day 03

Nairobi → Maasai Mara

Charter flight · Mara tented camp

Charter flight to the Mara. Children peer from the plane windows as the city gives way to the rift valley escarpment, then the open savannah below. Your family tented suite at the luxury camp has interconnecting rooms, child-safe nets, and a private terrace. First game drive in the afternoon — expect the children's first encounter with elephants to redefine "big."

  • Charter flight
  • Family suite arrival
  • First elephant encounter
  • Sundowner
Day 04

Junior Ranger Day

Maasai Mara · Junior Ranger Programme

The centrepiece family experience. Your guide delivers the Junior Ranger programme: tracking animals by footprint (you would be amazed how quickly children learn), learning birdcalls, studying insects, understanding the food chain from the ground up. The programme culminates in a formal Junior Ranger certificate ceremony and presentation.

  • Animal tracking lesson
  • Footprint identification
  • Bird and insect study
  • Junior Ranger ceremony
Day 05

Full Family Safari Day

Maasai Mara National Reserve

A full day tailored to your family's pace. The guide knows which game drives to keep moving (when young ones lose patience) and which to hold still (the lion kill that keeps everyone absolutely silent). Bush lunch in the field. Maasai village visit in the afternoon — children are entranced by the Maasai jumping game and the warriors' red ochre dress.

  • Full-day family game drives
  • Bush lunch in field
  • Maasai village visit
  • Village crafts for children
Day 06

Final Mara Morning → Nairobi

Mara · Charter · Departure

A final unhurried morning game drive — by now the children are identifying animals faster than the adults. Charter back to Nairobi. The Junior Ranger certificate gets framed when they get home. The stories never stop being told.

  • Final morning drive
  • Animal ID contest
  • Charter to Nairobi
  • Departure transfer
