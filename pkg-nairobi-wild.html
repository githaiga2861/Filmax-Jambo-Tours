The Journey

Lions With a City Skyline

The world's only capital city with a national park on its doorstep — and the wildlife is entirely real. Buffalo, rhinoceros, lion, cheetah, giraffe, zebra and over 500 bird species live within Nairobi National Park, and from the park's northern boundary the Nairobi city skyline is visible. There is nowhere else on earth where this is possible.

The Nairobi Wild break is designed for travellers with limited time who refuse to compromise on experience. Two days in and around Nairobi deliver more genuine wildlife encounters than most people receive in a lifetime.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Day 01

Nairobi National Park + Giraffe Centre + DSWT

Nairobi wildlife circuit

Morning game drive in Nairobi National Park — the world's only urban national park with free-ranging wildlife. The park regularly hosts all of the Big Five except elephant. Lion prides, rhino, and buffalo roam directly against the backdrop of the CBD skyline. Afternoon at the Giraffe Centre feeding Rothschild giraffe, then the 3pm feeding session at the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust baby elephant orphanage — one of Kenya's most compelling conservation stories.

  • NNP dawn game drive
  • Rhino and lion tracking
  • Giraffe Centre feeding
  • DSWT elephant feeding
Day 02

Nairobi Museum + Departure

Karen · Nairobi cultural circuit

Morning at the Nairobi National Museum and Snake Park, then the Karen Blixen homestead for those moved by Out of Africa. A Nairobi city market visit if time permits, and a Kenyan farewell lunch before airport or onward transfer.

  • Nairobi Museum visit
  • Karen Blixen homestead
  • City market option
  • Farewell Kenyan lunch
