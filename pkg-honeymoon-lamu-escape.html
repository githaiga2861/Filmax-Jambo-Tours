The Journey

The Most Romantic Island in East Africa — For Just the Two of You

Lamu does not hurry. There are no cars. The streets are too narrow and too ancient for them. Time moves by the tide and the call to prayer and the wind in the dhow sails. For a honeymoon, there is no more perfectly human-scaled destination in Kenya — perhaps in all of East Africa.

The Lamu Honeymoon Escape gives you five nights in a restored Swahili heritage mansion, a private rooftop terrace above the dhow harbour, a full day on a chartered dhow with your own crew, and the profound pleasure of doing absolutely nothing in one of the world's most beautiful places. This is the honeymoon for couples who know that luxury is not size — it is quality of attention.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Day 01

Nairobi → Lamu · Arrival

Wilson Airport · Lamu airstrip · Dhow transfer

Charter to Lamu airstrip on Manda Island. A private dhow transfers you across the channel to Lamu Island — the hotel porter navigating the narrow coral-stone alleys to your heritage mansion. Champagne awaits on the private rooftop terrace as the dhow harbour glows amber in the evening light.

Day 02

Lamu Old Town · Just the Two of You

Lamu Island

A private guided walk through Lamu Old Town — the oldest continuously inhabited settlement in East Africa. Carved Zanzibari doors, the 14th-century Lamu Fort, the Friday Mosque. Lunch at a waterfront rooftop restaurant, then the afternoon is entirely yours. Shela Beach — 12km of white coral sand — stretches before you.

Day 03

Private Dhow Day · Manda Island

Manda Channel · Indian Ocean

Your dhow and crew are yours for the entire day. Sail to Manda Island's uninhabited beaches — white sand and no footprints. Snorkel the coral reef off Manda Toto. The crew prepares a fresh seafood lunch on the beach with cold champagne. Sail back at sunset.

Day 04

Swahili Cooking for Two

Lamu Island · Private cooking class

A private Swahili cooking masterclass for just the two of you — a celebrated local chef teaches you to prepare pilau, coconut fish curry, and the Lamu sambuza. The morning market provides the ingredients. You eat what you cook together for lunch.

Day 05

Sea Kayak · Mangrove Forest

Lamu Archipelago · Mangrove channels

Morning sea kayak through the mangrove forest channels of the archipelago — a silent, bird-filled world that most visitors never see. Afternoon hammock and book at the hotel. Evening: your final rooftop dinner, the dhow lights reflected below.

Day 06

Final Morning · Departure

Lamu · Charter Nairobi

One final coffee on the rooftop as the harbour wakes up below. Dhow transfer to Manda airstrip. Charter to Nairobi with a Lamu-shaped space in your hearts.
