The Journey

Your Private Kenya. No Compromise.

Imagine Kenya entirely without strangers. No other vehicles. No shared sundowners. A conservancy bordering the Maasai Mara — with its own elephant herds, its own lion prides, its own leopards — bought out entirely for you.

The Private Conservancy Sojourn is the ultimate expression of Filmax Jambo Tours' philosophy: that the finest wildlife experiences happen when luxury and wilderness meet without interruption. Your dedicated private ranger, tracker, butler and chef attend to every dimension of the experience. This is Kenya as it existed before tourism — wild, intimate, and entirely yours.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Day 01

Arrival by Charter

Wilson Airport · Private Conservancy airstrip

Your private charter delivers you directly to the conservancy airstrip. No waiting, no other guests — your ranger and butler greet you at the aircraft steps. A welcome drive through your private wilderness before your tented villa is revealed. Champagne overlooking the plains as the first golden hour begins.

  • Private charter flight
  • Welcome drive
  • Villa orientation
  • Champagne sundowner
Day 02

Your Conservancy — Day 1

Private Mara Conservancy

Dawn game drive in an ecosystem that is entirely yours. Your ranger has been monitoring the lion pride's movements since the previous day. In the Mara ecosystem without vehicle competition, the big cats behave naturally — unguarded, unperturbed. Bush breakfast beside a termite mound. Afternoon helicopter flight option offers a completely different perspective of the conservancy.

  • Dawn lion drive
  • Bush breakfast
  • Helicopter flight (optional)
  • Guided bush walk
  • Private sundowner
Day 03

Walking Safari Day

Private Conservancy · Mara boundary

A full day on foot with your private ranger and armed tracker — the most intimate wildlife experience Kenya offers. Walking safari reveals what vehicles cannot: the ant colonies beneath your feet, the tracks that tell a story, the impala frozen five metres away, the sudden sprint of a hyena. Lunch served in the field beneath a fig tree. Evening: private in-bush dining experience — white linen, candlelight, and a hyena calling in the distance.

  • Full-day walking safari
  • Tracker-led tracking session
  • Field lunch
  • Private bush dinner
Day 04

Sleep-Out Night

Starbed Platform · Open Wilderness

The centrepiece experience of the Sojourn. As darkness falls, your butler accompanies you to a raised starbed platform deep in the conservancy. No tent, no walls — just you beneath the Kenyan sky, elevated above the ground, listening to the symphony of the African night. Hippos grunt below. Lions call in the distance. Dawn finds you having slept under more stars than you thought existed.

  • Sunset guided walk to starbed
  • Sleep-out night experience
  • Dawn wake with lion chorus
  • Private breakfast at starbed
Day 05

Helicopter & Mara River

Aerial game viewing · Mara River

Morning helicopter flight along the Mara River and over the open plains — this perspective reveals the sheer scale of the ecosystem and the patterns of wildlife movement visible only from altitude. Land at the Mara River for a private champagne picnic. Afternoon game drive focusing on leopard in the riverine forest.

  • Helicopter scenic flight
  • Mara River champagne picnic
  • Leopard tracking
  • Private farewell dinner
Day 06

Final Morning → Departure

Conservancy · Charter Nairobi

A last unhurried morning in your private kingdom. Breakfast wherever you choose — the ranger's favourite rock outcrop, the fig tree by the river. Then your charter returns you to Nairobi, carrying memories that require no photograph to last a lifetime.

  • Farewell morning drive
  • Breakfast of your choosing
  • Private charter departure
  • Nairobi transfer
