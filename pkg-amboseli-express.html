The Journey

Kilimanjaro at Dawn, Elephants at Dusk

The most iconic image in East African photography: a herd of elephants, dust-coated tusks sweeping low, moving across the plain beneath the snow-capped summit of Mount Kilimanjaro. Amboseli National Park is where this image is made real, every single morning.

Three days in Amboseli is a concentrated masterclass in one of Africa's most magnificent ecosystems. The park hosts some of the most studied elephant herds in the world, and the relationship between these elephants — who know this landscape across three generations — and the mountain that watches over them creates an atmosphere of extraordinary primordial calm.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Day 01

Nairobi → Amboseli

Charter or road · Amboseli arrival

Charter flight from Wilson Airport to Amboseli airstrip (45 minutes), or private road transfer through Emali with views of the Chyulu Hills. Arrival at your lodge — Kilimanjaro visible from the terrace, elephants already visible across the plain below. Afternoon game drive through the park's swamp circuit where elephant herds congregate in greatest numbers.

  • Charter or road transfer
  • Lodge arrival
  • Elephant swamp circuit
  • Kilimanjaro sunset view
Day 02

Full Amboseli Day

Amboseli National Park

Pre-dawn departure — Kilimanjaro is clearest before 8am. The mountain catches first light in shades of amber and rose as elephant families cross beneath its gaze. Bull elephants with tusks that touch the ground. Observation Hill at midday for a panoramic view of the entire ecosystem. Afternoon drive for cheetah and lion on the open flats. Sundowner on Observation Hill as the mountain catches the last orange light.

  • Pre-dawn Kilimanjaro dawn
  • Bull elephant tracking
  • Observation Hill midday
  • Cheetah drive afternoon
  • Sundowner Observation Hill
Day 03

Final Amboseli Morning → Nairobi

Amboseli · Departure

Final dawn drive — the elephants are on the move early, heading to the swamp. Breakfast at the lodge with the mountain visible. Charter or road transfer to Nairobi, arriving mid-afternoon.

  • Final dawn drive
  • Swamp elephant session
  • Lodge breakfast
  • Return to Nairobi
