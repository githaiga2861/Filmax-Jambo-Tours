Our Origin

A Love Letter
to Kenya

Filmax Jambo Tours was born from a single obsession: Kenya deserved a safari company as extraordinary as its wilderness. Founded by guides who grew up tracking lion in the Mara and counting flamingos on Nakuru's shores, we know this land in a way that cannot be learned from a brochure.

Our Promise

No Templates.
Only Journeys.

We refuse to run the same safari twice. Every itinerary we build is a bespoke composition — shaped by who you are, what moves you, and what you've never yet allowed yourself to dream. Luxury here is not thread-count. It is the quality of the silence between lion calls at 3am.

Our Standard

Excellence
Without Effort

From the moment you enquire to the evening you return home with dust still on your boots and wonder still in your eyes — every detail is attended to. Our concierge team operates around the clock. Your guide is not an employee; they are a storyteller, a naturalist, and a friend.
