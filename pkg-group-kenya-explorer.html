The Journey

The Complete Kenya — Together

Seven days across three of Kenya's most iconic destinations, designed for large groups who want the full Kenya story without the logistics headache. Corporate retreats, travel clubs, anniversary gatherings, university alumni — the Group Kenya Explorer handles the complexity so you handle the memories.

Two expert guides, a dedicated group coordinator who travels with you throughout, Nairobi wildlife introductions, Amboseli's elephant kingdom, and the Mara's open drama. Priced for groups of 8 to 20, with tiered pricing available. The more of you there are, the lower the per-person cost.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Day 01

Nairobi Welcome Day

Nairobi · Giraffe Centre · NNP

Group arrival day. Morning game drive in Nairobi National Park — lions against the city skyline is a fitting introduction to the Kenya the group is about to discover. Afternoon at the Giraffe Centre and DSWT elephant orphanage. Group welcome dinner at a Nairobi rooftop restaurant.

  • Nairobi National Park group drive
  • Giraffe Centre visit
  • DSWT elephant orphanage
  • Group welcome dinner
Day 02

Nairobi → Amboseli

Road transfer · Amboseli arrival

Convoy south through Emali with views of the Chyulu Hills. Arrival at Amboseli in the early afternoon. Kilimanjaro reveals itself above the treeline. First afternoon game drive — elephant herds moving beneath the mountain.

  • Convoy to Amboseli
  • Chyulu Hills viewpoint stop
  • Afternoon elephant drives
  • Group sundowner
Day 03

Full Amboseli Day

Amboseli National Park

Dawn to dusk in the elephant kingdom. Bull elephants with magnificent tusks, cheetah on the open plains, Observation Hill for panoramic views across the ecosystem. Group lunch at the lodge with Kilimanjaro visible from the terrace.

  • Dawn elephant drives
  • Big cat tracking
  • Observation Hill group visit
  • Group bush lunch
  • Sundowner drive
Day 04

Amboseli → Maasai Mara

Road transfer via Narok

Convoy northwest through Narok and into the Mara ecosystem. The landscape opens dramatically as you enter the reserve. Afternoon game drive. Group dinner at camp with a Maasai cultural performance arranged.

  • Full-day convoy with stops
  • Mara arrival afternoon drive
  • Group camp dinner
  • Maasai welcome performance
Day 05

Full Mara Day 1

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Split vehicles covering maximum ground. Dawn big cat drive coordinated by radio between the two guide teams. Bush breakfast in the field. Private conservancy afternoon. Group bonfire at camp.

  • Split vehicle dawn drives
  • Coordinated big cat tracking
  • Field bush breakfast
  • Private conservancy afternoon
  • Group bonfire evening
Day 06

Full Mara Day 2 · Bush Dinner

Maasai Mara · Bush dinner site

Final full safari day. Maasai village immersion in the morning. Full afternoon drives with maximum flexibility — follow what interests the group. The evening centrepiece: a private group bush dinner set up in the field with lanterns, long tables, and the Mara sky above.

  • Morning Maasai village visit
  • Full afternoon drives
  • Private group bush dinner in the field
  • Night drive
Day 07

Final Morning → Nairobi

Mara · Return convoy

A final dawn drive then convoy return to Nairobi, arriving mid-afternoon. The group coordinator assists with airport transfers and onward logistics.

  • Final group dawn drive
  • Camp breakfast
  • Return convoy to Nairobi
  • Airport and hotel transfers
