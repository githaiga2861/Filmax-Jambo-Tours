The Journey

Ancient Swahili Culture, White Coral Shores

Lamu is a world apart. The oldest continuously inhabited settlement in East Africa — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — Lamu Old Town has no cars, its narrow coral-stone lanes unchanged for eight centuries. Donkeys carry goods. Dhows sail by the same stars that guided Arab merchants in the 9th century.

The Lamu Archipelago offers a Kenya that has nothing to do with safari — an intimate encounter with one of the world's great living cultures, set against the backdrop of the Indian Ocean's most perfect islands. White coral beaches, dhow sailing, and a silence that is the rarest luxury of all.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Day 01

Nairobi Lamu

Wilson Airport · Lamu airstrip · Dhow transfer

Charter flight from Nairobi to Lamu airstrip on Manda Island, then the traditional dhow water crossing to Lamu Island itself. A porter navigates your bags through the narrow alleys to your heritage hotel. Afternoon walk through Lamu Old Town with a local guide.

Day 02

Lamu Old Town Deep Dive

Lamu Island

Lamu Fort, the Lamu Museum, the Friday Mosque — the layers of Swahili civilization revealed by your knowledgeable local guide. Lunch at a rooftop restaurant above the waterfront. Afternoon at leisure: the famous Shela Beach, white sand stretching 12km.

Day 03

Manda Island · Dhow Sail

Manda Channel · Indian Ocean

Private dhow for the day. Sail to Manda Island's uninhabited beaches. Snorkel the coral reef off Manda Toto island. The dhow crew prepare a fresh seafood lunch on the beach. Return at sunset.

Day 04

Swahili Cooking + Free Day

Lamu Island

Morning Swahili cooking masterclass with a celebrated local chef — learning to prepare pilau, biryani, coconut fish curry and the famous Lamu sambuza. The afternoon is yours completely.

Day 05

Final Morning Departure

Lamu · Charter Nairobi

One final coffee on the hotel rooftop above the dhow harbour. Dhow water transfer to Lamu airstrip, charter to Nairobi.
