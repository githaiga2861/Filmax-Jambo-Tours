The Journey

Pink Horizons, Rhinos and Rift Valley Drama

Lake Nakuru is one of the Rift Valley's great spectacles — an alkaline soda lake that at its peak hosted more than a million flamingos, turning the entire shoreline a vivid, impossible pink. The lake fluctuates with the rains, but even in quieter years the flamingo flocks number in the hundreds of thousands.

Beyond the flamingos, Nakuru National Park protects both black and white rhino — Kenya's strongest rhino population — alongside lion, leopard, giraffe, and waterbuck. The Baboon Cliff viewpoint surveys the entire lake from above. Three days is the perfect amount of time to absorb this underrated jewel of Kenya.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Day 01

Nairobi → Lake Nakuru

Naivasha en route · Lake Nakuru NP

Depart Nairobi west on the A104 through the rift valley. Stop at the Naivasha escarpment viewpoint — the visual drama of the valley from here sets the scene for the entire trip. Continue to Lake Nakuru National Park. Afternoon game drive with rhino as priority — Nakuru has over 100 rhino, the highest concentration in Kenya.

  • Naivasha viewpoint stop
  • Afternoon rhino tracking
  • Flamingo lake shore
  • Lodge sunset
Day 02

Full Nakuru Day

Lake Nakuru National Park

Dawn game drive along the lake shore. The flamingo spectacle is most dramatic at dawn when the light turns the water gold and the birds are most active. Move to the acacia forest for leopard (Nakuru has an excellent leopard population) and waterbuck. Baboon Cliff viewpoint at midday for aerial lake photographs. Afternoon — Rhino sanctuary internal track.

  • Dawn flamingo shore
  • Leopard forest drive
  • Baboon Cliff viewpoint
  • Rhino sanctuary afternoon
Day 03

Final Morning → Nairobi

Lake Nakuru · Return

Final morning game drive — often the most productive as the animals are least disturbed early in the day. Breakfast at the lodge with the lake visible from the terrace. Return to Nairobi, arriving mid-afternoon.

  • Final dawn drive
  • Lake terrace breakfast
  • Return to Nairobi
  • Transfer assistance
